# Spineplay 0.3.1 phone trial

Use a private test build and one Plex profile before inviting the family. These are the remaining live checks, not claims of completed testing.

1. Sign in through Plex in the phone's browser and return to the app. Confirm the correct Home profiles appear and a wrong protected-profile PIN is rejected.
2. Confirm a single available server is selected automatically. Select AudioBooks only on first setup. Confirm other libraries are excluded. In Settings, choose two permitted audio libraries, then return to one; verify the book list follows the selection.
3. Play a long M4B with named embedded chapters. Verify the selected chapter name and boundaries against the recording.
4. Move within that chapter. Verify whole-book progress changes coherently and **Undo jump** returns to the previous location.
5. Play a multi-file MP3 book. Seek across a file boundary in both directions and confirm there is one continuous book position.
6. Pause, close the app, reopen it. Confirm the chosen user/server/libraries are retained without asking for a PIN and the book is in Continue listening at the saved position. Repeat after force-stop and reboot.
7. Switch Plex profiles and open the same book. Verify the second profile has independent progress, history and bookmarks. Switch back and check the first again.
8. Lock the screen, use headphones, disconnect headphones, receive a call, and change Wi-Fi/mobile connectivity. Confirm playback and saved position behave sensibly.
9. Try speed, bookmarks, a short sleep timer, end-of-chapter sleep, and exact-time entry.
10. Change progress on another Plex client. Reopen the book in this app and check the resume-choice flow. Concurrent active devices need further conflict testing before family-wide use.
11. Search for a book and scroll. Open it, return to the library and confirm both the search and scroll are retained. Tap an author, inspect their other books, then return to the previous search.
12. Confirm new, recognised books appear above incomplete Plex disc entries. Confirm covers are displayed in full rather than cropped.
13. Open Unleashed. Check the 15-minute listening sections. In published chapter lookup, use UK edition B0D7MFC16J, review the 63-marker preview and apply it. Verify an early and late chapter against the recording; adjust opening-credit offset if needed. Confirm the current place and history do not move.
14. Download a multi-file book, interrupt Wi-Fi, resume and wait for Ready offline. Enable airplane mode, force-stop/reopen the app, then play and seek across file boundaries. Repeat after reboot. Check cached cover and chapter data remain available.
15. Test download pause, cancellation, retry after failure and removal. Confirm removing offline audio retains listening progress. Check notification behaviour when the app is backgrounded and screen locked. Check the unmetered-network preference before mobile-data use.
16. Switch profiles and confirm downloaded audio, progress and browsing preferences remain isolated. Reopen Settings and explicitly change users; the app must not bypass a protected profile's PIN on a new profile switch.
17. Install 0.3 over the old app without uninstalling. Confirm the launcher shows Spineplay with its own icon and remembered setup/progress/downloads survive. Check the brief silent intro, tap-to-skip, ordinary resume and the system's disabled-animation setting.
18. Use Finished, In progress, Not started and Listen later filters. Mark a book finished, check it leaves Continue listening and try Listen again. Confirm the previous position remains in History. Navigate from a search into a series, then an author, and return to the original search and scroll.
19. Open book details from the player cover and library more button. Check narrator/description/year against Plex, close while metadata is loading and confirm the sheet stays closed. Test series order including a decimal number, unknown order and a local correction. Confirm another profile's corrections remain independent.
20. Start a short bedtime timer, extend it, then test its final 15-second fade. Test end-of-chapter at a faster speed. Reopen the app and use Return to bedtime start; confirm the previous place is recoverable. Check the preferred duration follows the chosen profile.
21. Enable automatic backups in Settings and choose a real Android folder provider. Listen/pause and verify a readable backup appears. Repeat until three copies remain for that profile; unrelated files must remain untouched. Disconnect the provider or make the folder unavailable and check the error, retained copies and working local playback. Reconnect and use Back up now.
22. Export a backup, preview its restore and cancel once. Restore while an existing book has a different position: it should keep the phone's place and offer Backup alternative in History. Check a fresh installation/test profile recovery separately without uninstalling the only copy of valuable history. A different Plex user's backup must be rejected.
23. Listen offline, close/reopen and reconnect; verify pending Plex updates survive and clear. Move the same book on another Plex client before reconnecting. Review the two places, keep local once and use Plex once; confirm the actual player seeks to the selected place before queued updates resume. Try a deliberate backward seek and two simultaneously active devices, including profile changes during poor connectivity.
24. While book A is playing, tap book B's cover/title or Continue listening card. Confirm A's place is saved and B opens paused; only pressing Play starts B. Repeat for a downloaded book, a newer Plex-position choice and a finished-book reset. Explicit Resume buttons on the shelf and in details should still start playback; details for an already playing book must not pause it.
25. Reopen with a cached library and confirm it is usable while Plex is checked, then add a book on the server and relaunch or manually refresh. Repeat without network access and confirm the cached list remains. There should be no new continuous polling or background service for library refresh.

Suggested initial sample: Big Beacon, The Impossible Fortune, Source Code, My Family and Other Animals, and Trigger Mortis. They cover embedded chapters, chapter files, untitled markers and arbitrary splits. File/marker counts were sampled in the desktop Plex session; actual audio has not been modified.

Folder-provider behaviour, playback fades, real Plex sync races, background execution, lock-screen controls and system splash behaviour are device checks. The automated render/database/provider tests do not replace them. There is no new hosted service and no live cross-phone syncing of bookmarks or reading flags in this build.
